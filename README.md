# 🚀 Brick Rocket

A LEGO-style rocket building and flying game for kids. It runs in the browser and is made for iPad touch screens.

- **Build** your rocket brick by brick in the garage. Every brick costs coins. Tap a brick to take it back and get a full refund.
- **Fly** from Earth into space. The longer and higher you fly, the more coins you earn.
- **Collect** coins 🪙, repair kits 🔧, energy ⚡ and mystery crates ❓, which unlock new parts.
- **Avoid** meteors, or blast them with lasers ⚡, blasters 💥 and homing missiles 🎯. Press BARRIER 🔵 to smash through them and BOOST 🚀 to go faster.
- **Grow**: buy a bigger garage (up to 11×12) to build huge rockets.

## Controls
- **Touch:** put a finger anywhere and drag. The rocket turns that way and flies. The BOOST and BARRIER buttons are on the right.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD, Shift to boost, Space for the barrier.

## Develop
No build step. Serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) and open it in a browser. Run the tests with `npm test`.
